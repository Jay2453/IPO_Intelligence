# IPO Intelligence — Project Specification

## 1. Executive Summary & Vision
**IPO Intelligence** is an institutional-grade financial intelligence platform tailored specifically to the Indian Initial Public Offering (IPO) market (spanning Mainboard and SME platforms on NSE and BSE). 

Unlike generic stock-tracking apps that aggregate unverified grey market rumors or retail hype, **IPO Intelligence** is engineered on rigorous financial principles, strict data lineage, transparent provenance, and disciplined predictive modeling.

## 2. Core Product Principles
1. **Source Traceability & Ground Truth**:
   - All financial and issue data must trace back to authoritative, permissible filings: SEBI Draft Red Herring Prospectus (DRHP), Red Herring Prospectus (RHP), Final Prospectus, official exchange circulars (NSE/BSE), and statutory newspaper advertisements.
   - Every single observation must store publication timestamp, retrieval timestamp, canonical source URL, content hash (SHA-256), and extraction method.
2. **Strict Demarcation of Demo vs. Real Data**:
   - Synthetic or demonstration data used during testing or sandbox runs must be explicitly flagged with `is_demo: true` at the database level and prominently rendered with high-visibility `DEMO DATA` badges on the client interface.
   - Demo data seed mechanisms are strictly isolated behind environment guards (`ENABLE_DEV_SEED=true` and `APP_ENV != production`).
3. **Intellectual Honesty in Predictions**:
   - Scores are never described as "sure-shot gains" or "profit probabilities" unless generated by a validated, calibrated statistical or machine learning model.
   - All future predictions are immutable, append-only historical records tied to a specific `model_version`, feature snapshot, forecast horizon (e.g. `listing_day_close`, `listing_open`), and timestamp.
4. **Leakage Prevention**:
   - Historical model evaluation and training must strictly forbid future-information leakage. Features for pre-listing predictions must only utilize information available before the cut-off timestamp (e.g. IPO close date or allotment date). Listing day actual prices must never appear in pre-listing feature vectors.
5. **No Automated Trading / No Guarantees**:
   - The platform provides research, risk assessment, and decision support. It does not provide algorithmic execution, brokerage connectivity, or guarantees of financial returns.

## 3. IPO Lifecycle State Machine

The lifecycle of an Indian IPO follows a well-defined statutory progression governed by SEBI regulations, with legitimate non-linear transitions:

```
[ DISCOVERED ]
      │
      ▼
[ DOCUMENTS_PENDING ] ─────────────────────────┐
      │                                        │
      ▼                                        │
[ PRELIMINARY_ANALYSIS ] (DRHP available)      │
      │                                        │
      ▼                                        │
[ PRICE_BAND_AVAILABLE ] (RHP filed, dates set)│
      │                                        ▼
      ▼                                  [ WITHDRAWN ]
[ SUBSCRIPTION_OPEN ] (Bidding period)         ▲
      │                                        │
      ▼                                        │
[ SUBSCRIPTION_CLOSED ]                        │
      │                                        │
      ▼                                        │
[ FINAL_ANALYSIS ] (Basis of allotment, GMP)   │
      │                                        │
      ▼                                        │
[ LISTED ] (Trading commencement on NSE/BSE)   │
      │                                        │
      ▼                                        │
[ OUTCOME_VERIFIED ] ──────────────────────────┘
```

### Supported Lifecycle States:
- `DISCOVERED`: Initial public notice or filing rumors recorded from circulars or news.
- `DOCUMENTS_PENDING`: Awaiting DRHP or draft document upload to SEBI.
- `PRELIMINARY_ANALYSIS`: DRHP available; business model, historical financials, industry peers, and risk factors extracted.
- `PRICE_BAND_AVAILABLE`: RHP filed; price band (floor and cap), lot size, and issue opening/closing dates confirmed.
- `SUBSCRIPTION_OPEN`: Bidding period active (typically T to T+2 working days). Live subscription snapshots recorded per category (QIB, NII, RII, Employee).
- `SUBSCRIPTION_CLOSED`: Bidding window closed; cumulative demand known.
- `FINAL_ANALYSIS`: Final demand, issue price discovered, anchor book allocations analyzed.
- `LISTED`: Shares actively trading on secondary market (T+3 settlement listing).
- `OUTCOME_VERIFIED`: Actual listing day opening price, day-1 close price, and listing gain/loss verified against official exchange bhavcopy.
- `WITHDRAWN`: Issue cancelled or withdrawn by issuer/merchant bankers.
- `POSTPONED`: Issue deferred to a later date.

## 4. System Functional Modules

### 4.1 Data Ingestion & Provenance Engine
- **Source Observation**: Records raw HTTP responses, files, and circular metadata.
- **Document Management**: Manages versioned documents (DRHP, RHP, Addenda, Corrigenda, Basis of Allotment) with SHA-256 integrity hashes.
- **Ingestion Audit Log**: Audits all automated or manual fetch jobs, response codes, record counts, and errors.

### 4.2 Financial & Subscription Analysis
- **Financial Observations**: Normalized balance sheet, income statement, and cash flow metrics across Restated Financial Statements (typically 3 trailing fiscal years).
- **Valuation Metrics**: Post-issue P/E, P/B, EV/EBITDA, market cap at cut-off price, comparison against listed peer group.
- **Subscription Tracker**: Granular category-wise subscription demand snapshots (QIB, HNI/NII, Retail Individual Investors, Employees).

### 4.3 Market Observations
- **Grey Market Premium (GMP)**: Market sentiment observations sourced with attribution, disclaimers, and timestamp tracking.
- **Listing Day Performance**: Verified listing price, intraday high/low, closing price, traded volume, and percentage listing gain/loss.

### 4.4 Predictive Intelligence & Model Registry
- **Model Registry**: Catalogs model versions, architectures (e.g. Logistic Baseline, CatBoost Classifier), hyperparameters, validation scores (Brier Score, ROC-AUC), and training cutoff dates.
- **Predictions**: Append-only predictions of listing gain probability and risk decile.

## 5. Non-Functional Requirements
- **Performance**: Sub-100ms API response time for cached and indexed queries; sub-1.5s cold client page render.
- **Reliability**: Graceful fallback and honest empty states for unpopulated data fields.
- **Security**: Strict CORS configuration, parameterized queries (SQLAlchemy ORM), input sanitization (Pydantic), environment variable isolation.
- **Compliance**: Adherence to SEBI research analyst disclaimer mandates; explicit educational and analytical labeling.
